01 — Model accuracy

How good is the model?

Measured on data the model never saw during training: every day from 1 Jan 2025 to June 2026.

Reading these numbers honestly: price accuracy is high because Bitcoin rarely moves more than a few percent in a day, so "tomorrow ≈ today" is already a strong guess. The harder question is whether the price goes up or down, and there the model is right about half the time, the same as a coin flip. Treat this as a data-science study, not trading advice.

05 — How it works

From raw minutes to a forecast

01

Collect

7,616,181 one-minute OHLCV candles, Jan 2012 → Jun 2026. No missing values, no duplicates.

02

Resample

Grouped into 5,290 daily candles to cut the noise and keep the long-term trend.

03

Engineer

12 features from past prices only: lagged log-returns, distance from 7/30/90-day averages, volatility, momentum.

04

Train

Target is the next day's log-return. Trained on 2012–2024, tested on 2025–2026 with a strict time split.

05

Forecast

One step for past dates; for future dates, 100 Monte Carlo paths give a median and a 50% / 90% range.

Built with Python, scikit-learn, Flask, Three.js & GSAP.
